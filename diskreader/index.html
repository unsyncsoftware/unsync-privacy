DiskReader Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This policy describes how DiskReader, a Windows application by Unsync Software, handles information when you use the app.

Information DiskReader accesses

When you browse a disk, DiskReader reads the physical disk, partition information, file-system metadata, and file data needed to show the folders and files you select. This processing happens on your Windows device. DiskReader does not send disk contents, file names, or disk metadata to Unsync Software.

Read-only source disks and exports

DiskReader opens source disks for reading. It does not format, repair, or modify them. If you export files or folders, DiskReader writes the exported copies to the destination you choose. If you choose a cloud-synced destination, that service may sync the exported files under its own terms and privacy policy.

Administrator access

Windows may ask for administrator approval so DiskReader can access physical disks. The app runs with elevated permissions while it is open. Use it only with disks you are authorized to access.

Information sent to Unsync Software

DiskReader does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, or app telemetry. The app does not send your disk data to us. If you contact support, we receive the information you include in your message and use it to respond.

Microsoft Store

If you acquire DiskReader through the Microsoft Store, Microsoft handles Store account, purchase, payment, download, and update processes. Those processes are governed by Microsoft's Privacy Statement.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when DiskReader or its data practices change. The date at the top indicates the latest revision.

Contact
